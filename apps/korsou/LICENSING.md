# Terrain data licensing

The terrain assets under `assets/terrain/curacao/` are adapted from the
Copernicus DEM GLO-30 2021 release.

> produced using Copernicus WorldDEM-30 © DLR e.V. 2010-2014 and © Airbus
> Defence and Space GmbH 2014-2018 provided under COPERNICUS by the European
> Union and ESA; all rights reserved

Data DOI: <https://doi.org/10.5270/ESA-c5d3d65>

The source Cloud Optimized GeoTIFFs were accessed from the public Copernicus
DEM Registry of Open Data on AWS on 2026-08-07. The baker also accepts a
Copernicus GLO-30 GeoTIFF clipped by OpenTopography.

The procedural microterrain generated by the runtime is synthetic. It must not
be interpreted as measured geographic detail.

## Coastline data

The coastline source at `data/source/curacao-coastline-osm.json` is an
OpenStreetMap snapshot retrieved through the Overpass API on 2026-08-07. The
derived signed-distance field under `assets/terrain/curacao/` is built from OSM
ways tagged `natural=coastline`.

> © OpenStreetMap contributors

OpenStreetMap data is available under the Open Data Commons Open Database
License (ODbL) 1.0: <https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright>

## Place and waypoint data

The curated catalog at `assets/places.json` was retrieved from OpenStreetMap
through the Overpass API and Nominatim on 2026-08-10. Each geographic entry
retains its OSM element type and identifier. Relation and way coordinates are
their OSM search centres.

The catalog's area radii, specificity, waypoint order, and category selection
are Kòrsou presentation metadata. They are not OpenStreetMap boundaries and
must not be interpreted as legal, cadastral, or administrative limits.

> © OpenStreetMap contributors

OpenStreetMap data is available under the Open Data Commons Open Database
License (ODbL) 1.0: <https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright>
